# Evaluation report

_Generated by `python -m eval.run_eval --seed 7`. All numbers below were measured on this machine (Linux-6.18.44-fc-v185-x86_64-with-glibc2.39, Python 3.13.18, 2 CPU)._

## What was measured, and what it is not

- **Dataset**: 99 files (88 valid inputs + 11 deliberately broken), 134 labeled invoices. Formats: digital PDF, scanned PDF (clean/moderate/poor), PNG, JPEG, TIFF, XLSX, XLS, CSV.
- **Synthetic, not real-world.** Documents are generated from three invoice templates plus spreadsheet/CSV layouts, with exact labels. Real vendor invoices have many more layouts, so expect lower numbers on them. Treat these figures as a regression baseline and a measure of robustness to scan quality, *not* as a production accuracy claim.
- **Rule-based extraction only** (no LLM key was used). The LLM path is unit-tested with mocked responses; its accuracy was not measured.

## Performance and scale

- Whole dataset (99 files) processed sequentially in **18.8s**.
- OCR documents: 32 pages OCR'd, ≈ **0.55s per page** end-to-end per document (single document at a time).
- **1000-page digital PDF** (1.29 MB, one invoice per page): status `completed` in **2.7s** (375.3 pages/s), 1000 invoices found, 100.0% with all 8 fields exactly right, 157 flagged for review, peak RSS 377 MB.
- **40-page scanned PDF** (7.2 MB, moderate degradation) with 1 OCR worker(s): `completed` in **30.3s** (0.758s/page), 39 invoices, invoice number + total correct on 62.5%.
- **40-page scanned PDF** (7.2 MB, moderate degradation) with 4 OCR worker(s): `completed` in **20.1s** (0.501s/page), 39 invoices, invoice number + total correct on 62.5%.
- _Extrapolation, not measured:_ at 0.501s/page a fully scanned 1000-page PDF would take ≈ 8.3 minutes on this machine. Digital PDFs avoid OCR entirely.
